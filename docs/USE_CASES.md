# Enterprise Use Cases & Scenarios

The **Employee Attrition Root-Cause Intelligence** platform provides continuous workforce visibility and prescriptive action frameworks across key corporate challenges.

---

## 1. Seasonal Employee Contracts & Surge Workforce Management
### Scenario
E-commerce fulfillment centers, customer service contact centers, and retail organizations often experience high reliance on seasonal and short-term contract workers during Q4 or major promo seasons. Typically, these workforces suffer from elevated churn (30-50%) before contract termination dates, triggering costly labor shortages during critical revenue peaks.

### Platform Solution
- **Contract Volatility Diagnostics:** Tracks seasonal vs. full-time cohort risk trajectories and isolates burnout acceleration factors.
- **Milestone Retention Incentives:** Auto-recommends structured completion bonuses (e.g., 20% bonus paid on end-of-peak contract completion).
- **Early Conversion Pathways:** Identifies top quartile performers on seasonal contracts within 30 days and surfaces transparent offers for post-season full-time conversion.
- **Predictable Scheduling:** Flags teams with overtime exceeding 20 hours/month, advising HR to deploy temporary surge staff before core contract workers reach attrition triggers.

## 2. Reducing Turnover During Peak Seasons & Heavy Delivery Sprints
### Scenario
High-pressure engineering sprints, quarterly sales closings, and audit cycles regularly create intense workloads resulting in turnover spikes immediately following milestone releases.

### Platform Solution
- **Burnout Early-Warning Detection:** Analyzes the compound metric of weekly hours, consecutive weekend shifts, and work-life balance survey pulses.
- **Automated Workload Balancing:** Provides alerts when an employee crosses >25 overtime hours/month with a Work-Life Balance score $\le 2$.
- **Rest & Recharge Interventions:** Suggests mandatory recovery days and skip-level check-ins before disengagement crystallizes into departure notices.

---

## 3. Enhancing Employee Engagement & Combatting Career Stagnation
### Scenario
Tenured employees (2–4 years) who do not receive regular career advancement or lateral growth opportunities experience stagnation and seek outside alternatives, despite satisfactory compensation.

### Platform Solution
- **Mobility Bottleneck Detection:** Scans the interaction between tenure, `months_since_last_promotion`, and engagement scores.
- **Targeted Upskilling Playbooks:** Generates recommendations for executive mentorship, technical learning allowances, and cross-functional rotations.
- **Stay-Interview Prompts:** Alerts people leaders when tenure exceeds 24 months without promotional review, providing tailored conversation templates.

---

## 4. First-Year Integration & Onboarding Retention
### Scenario
Up to 25% of enterprise turnover occurs in the first 90 to 180 days due to unclear expectations, manager friction, or isolation in remote/hybrid environments.

### Platform Solution
- **Onboarding Risk Profiler:** Flags new hires ($<12$ months tenure) reporting low manager alignment or culture disengagement.
- **Structured 30-60-90 Interventions:** Prescribes automatic People Ops check-in sequences, buddy pairings, and project scope alignments to boost first-year tenure survival.
